# VULCAN OMNIPRO 220 — QUICK START GUIDE (Item 57812)

This single page gives the cable/polarity hookup for all four welding processes.

## STICK CABLE SETUP

[FIGURE stick-cable-setup | bbox: 30,110,860,470 | Stick hookup: ground clamp lead into the negative terminal, electrode holder lead into the positive terminal, wire feed power left disconnected.]

- 1. Connect ground clamp to **negative** terminal.
- 2. Connect electrode holder to **positive** terminal.
- 3. Leave wire feed power disconnected.

Callouts on the illustration: (1) points to the ground clamp lead entering the
lower-left socket on the machine's front panel; (2) points to the electrode
holder lead entering the socket to its right; (3) points to the electrode
holder itself. The ground clamp is drawn at the far left, the electrode holder
(red-tipped) below center.

## MIG CABLE SETUP

[FIGURE mig-cable-setup | bbox: 30,480,860,830 | MIG hookup: ground clamp into the negative terminal, wire feed power into the positive terminal, MIG gun onto the wire feed, wire feed control connected inside the welder.]

- 1. Connect ground clamp to **negative** terminal.
- 2. Connect wire feed power to **positive** terminal.
- 3. Connect MIG gun to wire feed.
- 4. Connect wire feed control inside welder.

Callouts: (1) ground clamp lead to the lower-left socket; (2) short jumper lead
from the wire feed into the positive socket at lower right; (3) the MIG gun's
cable attaching to the wire feed outlet; (4) the wire feed control lead entering
the machine above.

## FLUX CABLE SETUP

> Follow MIG cable setup but connect ground clamp to **positive** terminal and
> wire feed power to **negative** terminal.

(That is, flux-core reverses the MIG polarity.)

## TIG CABLE SETUP

[FIGURE tig-cable-setup | bbox: 30,845,860,1180 | TIG hookup: ground clamp into the POSITIVE terminal, TIG torch into the NEGATIVE terminal, gas line to the regulator, foot pedal connected inside the welder, wire feed power left disconnected.]

- 1. Connect ground clamp to **positive** terminal.
- 2. Connect TIG torch to **negative** terminal.
- 3. Connect gas line to gas regulator.
- 4. Connect foot pedal inside welder.
- 5. Leave wire feed power disconnected.

Callouts: (1) ground clamp lead into the lower-right socket (positive); (2) TIG
torch lead into the socket to its left (negative); (3) gas line running up to the
machine; (4) foot pedal lead entering the machine at the upper right; (5) the
ground clamp itself, drawn as a ring/clamp at lower center. The foot pedal is
drawn at the left, the TIG torch with its purple-banded collet at bottom center.
