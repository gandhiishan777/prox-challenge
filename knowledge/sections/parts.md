# Assembly Diagram

[FIGURE parts-exploded | bbox: 70,220,1120,1140 | Exploded assembly diagram of the whole welder with 61 numbered callouts keyed to the parts list; use it to identify a part number before ordering.]

An exploded isometric view of the complete welder with numbered leader lines
running to every component. Callout numbers **1 through 61** are printed in
circles around the perimeter of the drawing and keyed to the Parts List on the
facing page.

Reading the layout:

- **Top row (1–7 and 61):** the outer cover/wrap (7), side panel fasteners and
  brackets (5, 6), the carry handle assembly (3, 4), the main housing shell
  (1, 2), and at the far right the power cord with its plug (61).
- **Upper right (52–60):** the rear panel assembly — rear grille/frame (57, 58),
  the second power cord (55, 56), fan and fan guard (52, 53, 54), and the
  output socket hardware (59, 60).
- **Left column (16–25):** the MIG gun and its cable assembly (17, 18, 19), the
  front bezel and LCD window (20, 21, 22), and the front panel control knobs
  (23, 24, 25).
- **Center (8–15):** the internal chassis plate, the wire feed mechanism and
  drive assembly (12, 13, 14, 15), the spool holder (11), the main transformer
  and inductor (9, 10), and the internal fan (8).
- **Bottom row (26–43):** the front panel assembly and its fasteners (26–30),
  the electrode holder and ground clamp leads (31, 32, 33), internal wiring and
  the capacitor/board stack (34–39), and the heatsink assemblies (40–43).
- **Right column (44–51):** the IGBT/rectifier heatsink modules (44–51) mounted
  on the main power board.

Note: the numbered callouts identify part positions only; the corresponding part
numbers, descriptions and quantities are printed in the Parts List table.
