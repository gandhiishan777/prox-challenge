# VULCAN

## OMNIPRO 220

### MULTIPROCESS WELDING SYSTEM

[FIGURE omnipro-220-welder-photo | bbox: 260,1030,1420,1780 | Product photo of the VULCAN OMNIPRO 220 multiprocess welding machine, showing its front control panel, side vents, and front-panel connectors, illustrating the overall appearance and layout of the unit.]
- Top carry handle spans the top of the unit.
- Front control panel (left side of unit) includes:
  - Small "VULCAN" logo in upper left of the panel.
  - LCD display screen showing "MIG Steel C25" with a torch icon and a row of small icons/menu items below it.
  - Knob labeled "HOME" to the lower left of the display.
  - Knob labeled "BACK" to the lower right of the display.
  - Three round control knobs below the display; the rightmost knob has a "V" label above it, and the leftmost has a small icon to its left.
  - "VULCAN" logo printed on a black bar below the knobs.
  - Below that, a recessed panel area containing a square opening (upper left) and a toggle/rocker power switch (to its right), with air vent louvers to the right of the switch.
  - Three cylindrical connector fittings (torch/gas/cable connectors) along the bottom edge of the front panel.
- Right side panel of the unit (visible in perspective) shows:
  - A vertical row of angled air vent louvers.
  - "OMNIPRO 220" logo badge in the upper right area of the side panel.
  - "VULCAN" logo printed below the vent louvers on the side panel.
- The whole unit sits on a dark base/wheel assembly visible at the bottom of the photo.
